Three Rules for Returning Home

Dear traveler,

Before you step aboard, keep these three rules close. The sea rewards more than courage.

Respect your hosts: An open cave is not an invitation to enter.

Look after the crew: A clever plan succeeds when everyone works together.

Keep your pride in check: Surviving danger matters more than claiming credit for it.

May you find your way home,
Your Captain
